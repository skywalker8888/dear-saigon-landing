How it might work

A quieter phone. A warmer welcome.

Sketch of the flow. Nothing is wired up today — pick up the phone the normal way to reach the team.

Step 01

Someone calls Dear Saigon

The kitchen is slammed, the team is running food, and the phone rings. Today, sometimes we can't get to it fast enough.

Step 02

The assistant listens

In the concept, a friendly voice greets the caller and figures out whether they need hours, directions, a table, or a real person.

Step 03

The team follows up

Any request that needs judgment — an allergy note, a large booking, catering — comes to the restaurant team to confirm personally.

Concept FAQ

Straightforward answers about this page.

Just so it's clear what this is — and what it isn't — today.

Is this a live service? Can I use it now?

No. This is a concept preview. There is no AI answering the Dear Saigon phone today. To reach us, please call (905) 633-8388 during open hours, or visit us at 2405 Fairview St.

How do I actually place an order or reserve a table?

The best way is to call the restaurant directly, or drop in. Online options can vary — please check the home page for anything currently available.

What are your hours?

Hours change with holidays and season — please confirm with the restaurant. You'll find our current hours on our home page, or you can call us to check.

Why are there no prices or exact times on this page?

On purpose. Prices and timing are always confirmed with the restaurant — this page is only a design sketch, not a menu or an ordering system.

Will this ever become a real feature?

Maybe. We're exploring how a phone assistant could help during busy service without losing the warmth of a real Vietnamese noodle bar. If we ship it, we'll say so clearly on the home page first.
