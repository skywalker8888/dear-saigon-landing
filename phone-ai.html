What we're exploring

A phone assistant — someday, maybe.

The phone rings a lot at Dear Saigon. We're sketching what it might look like if a warm, friendly assistant could help pick up the busy calls — so the team can keep cooking. This section is a concept. It is not a live product, and calling us today still gets you a real person at the restaurant.

Common questions

Straightforward answers.

How do I order?

Call the restaurant at (905) 633-8388 to place a pickup order. Tell us what you'd like and we'll confirm a pickup time. You're also always welcome to walk in at 2405 Fairview St.

Is the phone assistant on this page live?

No. This page is a concept preview. There is no AI answering the Dear Saigon phone today — a real person picks up during open hours.

Can I reserve a table?

Yes — give us a call and we'll do our best to fit you in. For larger groups, calling ahead is the reliable way.

What are your hours?

Hours change with holidays and season. Please confirm with the restaurant — you'll find current hours on our home page, or you can call.

Why are there no prices on this page?

On purpose. Prices live on our menu (PDF), and totals are always confirmed by the team when you order.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes — a few. Give us a call and we'll walk you through what's on tonight.
