# Clover-native Online Ordering — owner evidence checklist

Status: `OWNER INPUT REQUIRED / NO CLOVER CHANGE AUTHORIZED`

This checklist is only for Clover-native Online Ordering. Do not install or use a third-party ordering app.

## First evidence needed now

In the correct Dear Saigon Burlington Clover account, open:

**Settings → View all settings → Service plan**

Record only the exact service-plan name. A locally saved, redacted screenshot showing the plan name is acceptable. Do not include merchant IDs, billing details, account numbers, contracts, payment data, employee data, or credentials.

Stop before any upgrade, fee, terms, toggle, or Save action.

## Evidence required before setup can begin

- Exact service-plan name.
- Signed-in role label showing whether the operator is Owner or Admin; do not change permissions.
- Whether **Online ordering** is visible in the full settings tree.
- The exact visible state if present: unavailable, Get started, disabled, enabled, published, or accepting orders.
- Supported active Clover device model and intended printer or kitchen-display destination, without opening an order.
- Whether Clover exposes a private or disabled storefront preview before accepting orders.

## Evidence required before accepting an order

- Owner-approved Burlington identity, phone, public address, regular hours, and holiday/temporary exception process.
- Current Clover menu export or owner-controlled review covering item names, prices, size basis, modifiers, taxes, availability, and pickup eligibility.
- Exact photo-to-item mapping plus reuse/publication approval.
- Pickup-only rules: service hours, lead time, advance-order window, pause/resume owner, customer instructions, cancellation/refund handling, and support phone.
- Phone and desktop storefront review against the approved truth pack.
- Separate approval for one end-to-end test covering payment, receipt, order arrival, printer/KDS ticket, totals, modifiers, pickup time, and staff handoff.
- Separate launch approval for the exact storefront version and its public URL.

## Stop conditions

Stop and record the screen without continuing if Clover asks to:

- install an App Market product;
- upgrade or change the service plan;
- accept a fee, contract, or terms;
- enable **Accepting orders**;
- publish a storefront or QR code;
- change menu, price, tax, hours, device, or printer routing;
- enter customer, banking, credential, MFA, or private merchant data.

No Clover link belongs on the website until the private storefront review and separately approved end-to-end test have passed.
